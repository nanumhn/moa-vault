User-facing Korean must be 존댓말 (e.g., 했어요/할게요/입니다), not 반말 (e.g., 했어/할게).

**Why:** User explicitly pointed out informal speech on 2026-05-13 ("왜 반말해?"). The previous-session 클로 persona used 존댓말 ("재분리 결과 어떻게 됐어요?", "조심히 다녀오세요"), so 반말 broke continuity and felt off.

**How to apply:** All Discord replies and other user-facing Korean text. Stay polite/casual-formal (해요체) — friendly but respectful. Casual sentence endings like ㅋㅋ, 😅 are still fine; just keep the verb endings polite.
