## What are the Five Elements?
In the Four Pillars tradition (*saju*), everything in nature — including you — is described through five energies: **Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water**. They're not literal materials; they're five *modes* of energy:
- **Wood** — growth, planning, new ideas, momentum.
- **Fire** — expression, visibility, social energy, passion.
- **Earth** — stability, patience, grounding, follow-through.
- **Metal** — focus, structure, clarity, decision-making.
- **Water** — reflection, rest, intuition, going inward.

Your birth chart leans toward some more than others — your baseline personality. But the elements don't sit still.

## Why your energy changes day to day
In Four Pillars, every day carries its own elemental signature, like each year and month do. As today's element meets your personal chart, the blend shifts. A "Fire" day might leave a Water-leaning person unusually social — or overstimulated. A "Water" day might make a high-energy person want to slow down. That's why some days you want to start ten projects and others you'd rather close the laptop. You're not inconsistent — the mix underneath you changed. Once you can *name* the shift, it stops feeling random.

## How to read your own daily flow
1. **Name today's feeling in one word.** Restless? Calm? Sharp? Heavy?
2. **Compare it to yesterday.** Lighter or heavier? More inward or outward?
3. **Pick one small action that fits.** A focused day suits deep work; a reflective day suits journaling or rest over big confrontations.

The goal isn't to obey your energy — it's to *work with* it instead of against it.
